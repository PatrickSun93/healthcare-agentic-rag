# Hypertension Management — Quick Guideline

> SYNTHETIC demo content for a RAG pipeline. Not medical advice. Contains no real PHI.

## Blood Pressure Categories
- Normal: less than 120/80 mmHg.
- Elevated: systolic 120-129 and diastolic less than 80 mmHg.
- Stage 1 hypertension: systolic 130-139 or diastolic 80-89 mmHg.
- Stage 2 hypertension: systolic greater than or equal to 140 or diastolic greater than or equal to 90 mmHg.

## First-Line Pharmacotherapy
- General population: a thiazide diuretic, ACE inhibitor, ARB, or calcium channel blocker.
- Patients with both diabetes and hypertension: an ACE inhibitor or ARB is the preferred first-line agent, especially in the presence of albuminuria.

## Follow-up
Recheck blood pressure within 4 weeks of starting or changing antihypertensive therapy.
