# Metformin Extended-Release — Prior Authorization Policy

> SYNTHETIC demo content for a RAG pipeline. Not medical advice. Contains no real PHI.

## Covered Indication
Metformin extended-release (ER) is covered for adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10 E11.9).

## Prior Authorization Criteria
Approval requires ALL of the following:
1. Documented diagnosis of type 2 diabetes (ICD-10 E11.x).
2. Most recent HbA1c greater than or equal to 6.5% within the last 90 days.
3. Trial of, or documented contraindication to, immediate-release metformin (step therapy).
4. eGFR greater than or equal to 30 mL/min/1.73m^2. Metformin is contraindicated below an eGFR of 30.

## Quantity Limits
Maximum 2000 mg per day. Immediate-release metformin must be tried before extended-release unless intolerance is documented.

## Renewal
Reauthorization is required every 12 months and must include a repeat HbA1c and a repeat eGFR.
