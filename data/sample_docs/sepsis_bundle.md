# Sepsis 1-Hour Bundle — Protocol Summary

> SYNTHETIC demo content for a RAG pipeline. Not medical advice. Contains no real PHI.

## Trigger
Initiate the sepsis protocol when infection is suspected AND lactate is greater than 2 mmol/L, or there is evidence of organ dysfunction.

## One-Hour Bundle
Complete ALL of the following within one hour of recognition:
1. Measure lactate; remeasure if the initial lactate is greater than 2 mmol/L.
2. Obtain blood cultures before administering antibiotics.
3. Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics.
4. Begin rapid crystalloid fluids at 30 mL/kg for hypotension or a lactate greater than or equal to 4 mmol/L.
5. Apply vasopressors if the patient remains hypotensive to maintain a mean arterial pressure (MAP) greater than or equal to 65 mmHg.

## Escalation
Reassess volume status and tissue perfusion after the bundle. Escalate to the ICU if vasopressors are required.
